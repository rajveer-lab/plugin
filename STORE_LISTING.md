## Detailed Store Description

Check AI answers as you read them. Hallucination Guard works with **ChatGPT (chatgpt.com)**, **Claude (claude.ai)**, and **Gemini (gemini.google.com)**, and can also check text you choose from any website.

Each report separates information into three parts:

- **Confirmed:** supported by the sources checked.
- **Hallucinated:** conflicts with sources.
- **Unverified:** no source was found. Unverified information is never counted as hallucinated.

Use the popup's paste box to check any text, or select text on a page and choose **“Fact-check with Hallucination Guard”** from the right-click menu. The report shows the checked sentences and the evidence behind the results.

### Features

- See a percentage breakdown of confirmed, hallucinated, and unverified information in AI answers and text you submit.
- Flag links in AI answers that lead nowhere, or to pages that say something else.
- Hover any checked sentence to see the source behind its result.
- Check facts against Wikipedia and exact Wikidata facts, including places, rivers, capitals, and who made a work.
- On computers that support Chrome's built-in AI, a model on your own device judges claims against the sources. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.
- Fact memory remembers claims already proven true or false, so repeats are flagged instantly. You can review and delete it in Settings.

### Privacy

Checks run in your browser. Wikipedia and Wikidata lookups send the relevant search terms to those services. Optional sources are controlled in the extension settings. No account is required.
